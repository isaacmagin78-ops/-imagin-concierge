SPACEX (SPCX)

2026–2028 TRAJECTORY
Market Perspective
2002–2025|The Monopoly Earned

Reusable boosters collapsed the cost of reaching orbit and made SpaceX the default ride to space. Starlink then converted that launch capability into a recurring consumer subscription — a deep-technology company that built its own customer instead of waiting for one.

Feb–Jun 2026|Daylight

An xAI merger folded an artificial-intelligence business into the rocket company at a combined $1.25 trillion. Four months later the June 12 listing raised roughly $75 billion — the largest IPO on record — and ended the company's ability to choose what it disclosed.

2026–2028|The Proving Ground
Starship
V3 flying — flights 12 and 13 cleared. Cadence must now carry the V3 satellites, due to begin launching late 2026.
Starlink
12 million subscribers across 164 countries, a record 1.7 million added in Q2. The engine funding everything else.
AI Capex
$15.8 billion of $18.4 billion quarterly capital spend. The AI unit grew revenue 247% and still lost $1.3 billion.
Float
Lockup opened 6 August on up to 911.5 million shares. Supply meets a stock still below its June peak.

Selected reported points — not a continuous series
Bottom Line

Reusable launch and Starlink built a business the public market will pay for. Whether $15.8 billion a quarter of AI infrastructure becomes the next Starlink or a permanent drag is now the whole question — and it gets answered in public every ninety days.
