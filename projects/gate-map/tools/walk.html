Walk sheet

Fill this in as you walk the property. It saves as you go — you can close the phone and come back. When you're done, hit the yellow button and paste the result into Claude.

Step 1 · Before you start walking

Do this one thing first Open Google Maps, find your community, switch to satellite, zoom so the whole property fills the screen, and screenshot it. That single image gives Claude the road shape — far more accurate than describing turns. Send it along with this sheet.

Step 2 · Stand at the gate, then drive/walk the loop

Order matters Add buildings in the order you actually pass them coming in from the gate. That order tells Claude the layout even if the satellite shot is fuzzy.

Step 3 · Don't forget

Easy to miss on the walk Package room or parcel lockers · Leasing / HOA office · Mailboxes · Dumpsters · Guest parking · Any building whose entrance faces away from the road · Any dead end or gate that looks like a through-road but isn't.
