# Arturo's channel — documentary concept

**The instinct is right, and it's the stronger of the two ideas.** Isaac already has the
production capability, Arturo already has the archive, and a YouTube channel needs no
approval from anyone. Nobody has to say yes before it starts.

## Why this works when a hunting channel normally wouldn't

Hunting content on YouTube is saturated — endless kill-shot compilations competing on the
same 30 seconds. That lane is closed.

**What Arturo has that nobody else can get is decades of archive and a place with a
documented history.** Legends Ranch, established 1998, Bitely MI. That is not content
anyone can replicate this year. It's the moat.

The already-verified material in `legends-ranch/deliverables/`:
- Arturo's own quote and the **83% return-rate stat** (in the anthem film)
- The **Wildlife Center**: 24,000 sq ft, 3,000+ mounts, **5 of the top-10 SCI records**,
  free student field trips, **Purple Heart Hunts** — a 501(c)(3) with a real mission
- A finished 64-second anthem and a finished 52-second Wildlife Center film

That's a documentary subject, not a hunting channel. The difference is the whole pitch.

## The angle

**Not** "watch us hunt." **Instead:** the story of a place, a man, and what he built —
told through an archive nobody else has.

Purple Heart Hunts alone is a documentary. Veterans, a ranch that hosts them for free, and
footage going back years. That travels far outside the hunting audience, which is exactly
what you want for reach.

## Format — start narrow

**One recurring series, not a channel of everything.** Pick a single format and produce
ten of them before adding a second.

Strongest candidate: **archive-driven short episodes, 3–6 minutes.** One story per
episode, built from Arturo's photos and old footage, with him narrating in his own voice.
The Ken Burns approach — stills, movement, a voice, restraint. This suits the material
because most of the archive is *photos*, and photos are the format's strength, not its
limitation.

Cross-post the first 45 seconds of each as a vertical short to Instagram, TikTok, and
YouTube Shorts. That's already how the engine works.

## Name ideas
- **Legends Ranch Stories** — plain, obvious, and it makes the ranch the brand
- **Bitely, Michigan** — place-as-title; documentary tone
- **Est. 1998** — from the site's own footer, works as a series card

## What to ask Arturo for

Ask for exactly this, in this order:

1. **The archive.** Photos and any old video, however disorganized. A shoebox is fine.
   The mess is not a problem — sorting it is the work.
2. **Him talking.** Not a script. Sit him down and ask him to tell one story. Record it
   on a phone. That's the whole audio track for episode one.
3. **His Part 2 quote from the anthem, re-recorded in his own voice.** It's already his
   words being read by a placeholder TTS voice. His voice replaces it and the film gets
   noticeably better for free.

Asking him for the archive also does something useful beyond the footage: it makes him a
participant rather than a subject. People back things they're inside of.

## Why this is worth doing even though YouTube monetization is far off

The channel is currently at **18.3 of the 4,000 watch hours** needed for the Partner
Program. Ad revenue is not the reason to do this — and pretending otherwise would be the
same mistake that's been made elsewhere in this business.

The real reasons:
1. **It's a portfolio piece with a real client.** Nothing sells outdoor video work like
   outdoor video work.
2. **It compounds with the Fish and Hunt USA pitch.** "Here's a documentary series I
   produce for one of your clients" is a stronger opener than any deck.
3. **Longform builds the watch hours** that shorts never will — Shorts views don't count
   toward the 4,000-hour threshold. If YouTube monetization is ever going to happen, this
   is the only content type that gets there.
4. **The Wildlife Center is a 501(c)(3)** with an existing donate CTA. A documentary
   series pointed at a nonprofit is a fundable thing, and the fundraising playbook is
   already written in `legends-ranch/site-audit/`.

## First step

One episode. Built entirely from material Arturo already has, narrated by Arturo, cut in
the existing engine. Ship it before designing a channel, a schedule, or a brand.

If it lands with him and his family, everything else follows. If it doesn't, one episode
is a cheap thing to have learned from.
