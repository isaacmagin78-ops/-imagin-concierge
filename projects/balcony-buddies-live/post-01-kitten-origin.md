# Post 01 — The Kitten Origin Story

**The launch piece.** Uses photos that already exist. No cameras, no OBS, no
waiting. This is the first Balcony Buddies content and it can go out tonight.

> ✅ **The cat is Calico.** Emilio and his wife, unit 203, seven floors below.
>
> Captions below are written to use the name instead of pronouns — so they ship
> correctly whether Calico is male or female. An earlier transcript said "his
> eyes," but calico coats are almost always female, so the question is open and
> the copy simply avoids it.

---

## Why this one first

An eight-month transformation with a complete photo record is the strongest
asset identified so far. Rescue-origin content performs because the payoff is
built in — you open on something fragile and end on something thriving. The
audience does the emotional work themselves.

It also introduces the premise before the live stream exists, so the feed
launches to people who already know the cast.

---

## Format

Photo reel, chronological, 20–30 seconds. Oldest photo first. Hold the opening
frame about a second longer than the rest — that image is the hook and it needs
a beat to land.

Pace: quick through the middle, slow on the last shot.

**The final frame must be him on the balcony.** That shot is the whole
concept — it's where the name came from, and it sets up everything that follows.

---

## Text overlays

| When | Text |
| --- | --- |
| Opening frame | `Found in Miami. Couldn't open his eyes yet.` |
| Early photos | `Week 1` |
| Through the middle | `Week 3` → `Month 2` → `Month 4` |
| Balcony shot | `Today. Eight months old.` |
| Final beat | `He watches the pool now.` |

Keep them short. The photos carry it.

## Where it goes

**Instagram Reels first.** Photo-reel storytelling performs best there and it's
where this format is native.

**TikTok second**, same day or next. Trim the caption — TikTok wants the hook
faster:

```
Found in Miami. His eyes weren't even open yet. Eight months later
he's got a balcony and a view of the pool 🐈

#rescuecat #kittenrescue #catsoftiktok #adoptdontshop #balconybuddies
```

**Threads**, text-only with one photo — the before shot and the today shot:

```
Neighbor found a kitten in Miami with his eyes still shut. Eight months
later he has a preferred balcony chair and strong opinions about who's
at the pool.

He lives eight floors below my pit bull. Same stack. Whole building is
rescues at this point.
```
