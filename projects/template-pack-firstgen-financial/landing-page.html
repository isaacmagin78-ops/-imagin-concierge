Nobody explains this to you

If you didn't go to college yourself, there's no one at the kitchen table who's seen this before. Counselors are split across hundreds of students. The colleges have no reason to explain the parts that cost you money.

So you're handed a document full of words like subsidized, SAI, and cost of attendance, given a few weeks to decide, and expected to make one of the largest financial decisions of your life.

This is the plain-English version. What to do, what month to do it, and exactly where families lose money.

The one thing most families never do

You are allowed to ask a college for more money. It has a name — a special circumstances review — and financial aid offices process them every year as ordinary business.

Almost no first-generation family asks, because nobody tells them it's permitted. The toolkit shows you when you have a real case and gives you the letter to send. That single section can be worth more than four years of the subscription.
