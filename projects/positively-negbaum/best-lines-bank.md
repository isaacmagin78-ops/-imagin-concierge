# Best Lines Bank — Isaac's verbiage, preserved verbatim
Use these as source material. Do not paraphrase or "improve" without labeling the edit.

## Identity / Name
- A name can echo. Especially underwater.
- Maybe they wanted to defame my name because I wasn't the same.
- They didn't like the cut of poor Wesley's jib.
- Your choices shape your fate, not your name.
- You are not the rumor. You are not their blame.
- I carry the name, but I choose what it means.
- Names are just seeds. Choices bloom true.
- Not because he changed it. Because he owned it.
- Not because they approved. Because he stopped asking.
- I'm absolutely positive.

## Sourpusses / Negativity
- The Salty Sourpusses joined the fray.
- The rumors swirled and twirled through the sea.
- Loose lips sink ships, and those lips let it rip.
- They blamed and defamed, then blamed him again.
- They whined and complained and played the blame game.
- Who's Got It Worse? Who's first at being the worst?
- They never grew. They stayed the same.
- The winner got absolutely nothing. Except attention.
- First of the worst. A school of fools swimming in circles.
- Got me over a barrel / shanghaied so badly / no one would touch me with a ten-foot pole.
- Groggy, and grumbled and groaned, with the unhappiest tone.
- Lost at sea, adrift in dire straits, out of my depth, off the deep end.

## Nautical / Course / Choice
- His choices determine the ship's course.
- Loose lips may sink ships, but wisdom equips.
- Their words are the weather. Your will is the wheel.
- Stay shipshape, young Wesley. Keep even keel.
- Why fight the current when you can ride the wave?
- Anchors away. We seize the day.
- A ship in the harbor won't learn from the wind.
- A good captain steers through the blame.
- Choose the good current.
- Ship shape and first rate / always keep it reel / the coast is clear / show a leg.
- Through thick and thin, till the bitter end, but never overwhelmed. Unlike a fair-weather friend.

## Humor / Parent Winks
- Which is convenient. And also nonsense.
- Seaweed crumble. Obviously.
- Somewhere nearby, a shrimp dropped his lunch.
- Manatees float. So that didn't work. At all.
- Tiny word surgery. Useful, oddly.
- Even the crab said, "Annoyingly nice."
- "My socks are not wet." … "Wait. We're fish. That's the best one yet."
- Being a manatee is terrible training for being a clam.
- Gratitude? In public? How deeply unfair.
- Crabby looking almost happy, which is probably illegal.

## Happy / Marcus
- Real friends don't cheer when you swim off track.
- If they loved you, they'd lift you. If they dim your light, ditch it.
- I clam up, then clamp down. Click-clack, just like that. (Clampdown Protocol)
- Not every loud fish deserves your ear.
- A thought can drift past without being true.
- The obstacle's the way.
- Still water grows stale. Storms polish your shell.
- Grit turns to pearl. And pearls turn out well.
- Truth without kindness is not really love.
- Safety is good. And courage is too. The wise heart knows which one to do.
- Your shell is your friend. But shells are beginnings, not where you must end.
- A little each day can grow quite a lot, like a tidal wave born from a single drop.
- Good. Better. Best. (the game that replaces Who's Got It Worse?)
- Neg be gone with Negbaum's!
