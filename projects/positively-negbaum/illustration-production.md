# Illustration Production Plan — Positively Negbaum, Book #1

## THE KEY FINDING (read this first)

The master manuscript runs 52 pages. **Your storyboards run 27 panels.** The storyboards
are not a rough version of the manuscript — they are the *edit*. You already made the cuts.

27 story panels is almost exactly a standard 32-page picture book:

| Pages | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Half title |
| 2–3 | Title spread + copyright |
| 4–30 | Story, 27 panels, one per page |
| 31 | Closing moral |
| 32 | Back matter / about |

**Decision needed from Isaac (one call, blocks nothing else):** produce the 27-panel
storyboard version as the book, and keep the 52-page master as the read-aloud / extended
text. Recommended: yes. 27 panels is publishable picture-book length; 52 is not — it's
double the standard, roughly double the print cost, and past the attention span for the
3–7 age band.

Everything below is built for the 27-panel version.

---

## STYLE BIBLE (locked from the three concept sheets)

**Medium:** Soft painterly digital illustration. Visible brush texture, no hard vector
edges, no flat cartoon fills.

**Light:** Warm underwater light from above. Golden god-rays through blue-green water.
Soft glow on faces. Darker, cooler water in the gloom scenes; brighter, warmer, more
saturated in the hope scenes.

**Palette:**
- Water: teal, aqua, deep marine blue
- Coral/reef: warm coral pink, orange, mustard, violet
- Wesley: soft blue-grey
- Accents: golden light, pearl cream, warm lamp-yellow for interiors
- Gloom scenes: desaturated, greener, murkier
- Hope scenes: brighter, higher contrast, more gold

**Character shapes:** Rounded, soft, friendly. No sharp threatening silhouettes even on
the villains — the Sourpusses are funny-grumpy, not scary.

**Signage/type in art:** Hand-painted wooden signs, scroll parchment, bubble lettering.
Signs carry the parent-facing jokes.

**Emotional rule:** Wesley's posture is the story. Droops and shrinks in pages 4–14,
straightens and rises from page 19 on. Track it deliberately across the book.

## CHARACTER BIBLE (lock these — every prompt references them)

**WESLEY NEGBAUM** — Young manatee. Soft blue-grey skin with subtle mottling. Large
round head, gentle rounded snout with small whisker bristles, big dark expressive eyes
with bright catchlights, small rounded flippers, wide paddle tail. Perpetually sweet-
faced even when sad. No clothing. Roughly the proportions of a chubby toddler.

**HAPPY THE CLAM** — Small scalloped clam. Tan-cream ridged exterior, warm pink-cream
mother-of-pearl interior with a soft glow. A small round face sits inside the shell:
big eyes, tiny smile. Very expressive despite having no body. Opens and snaps shut with
a visible CLACK. Sits in the sand, roughly the size of Wesley's head.

**MARCUS A. WHALEUS** — Large deep blue-grey whale. Kind heavy-lidded eyes, calm faint
smile, weathered wise face. Wears a draped robe/mantle across his back with a round
medallion at the front. Moves slowly. Fills the frame when he appears — scale is the
point. Lit like moonlight even underwater.

**THE SALTY SOURPUSSES** — A cluster of 5–8 grumpy fish. Grey-green and dull blue.
Heavy scowling brows, downturned mouths, half-lidded eyes, puffy cheeks. Theatrical
misery, not menace. Always appear as a crowd/cloud, never alone.

**THE YEAH-YEAHS** — Small overexcited fish in matching sunglasses and little caps.
Bright yellow and orange. Always mid-cheer, always at a safe distance from the danger
they're encouraging.

**NEGATIVE NELLY** — Purple jellyfish. Droopy bell, trailing tentacles, half-lidded
bored expression. Always drifting slightly behind everyone else.
> **Consistency flag:** across your three sheets the purple jellyfish appears in the
> Yeah-Yeah panel. Canon says Nelly is a separate character from the Yeah-Yeahs. Fix
> at production: **Yeah-Yeahs = the sunglasses fish crew. Nelly = the purple jellyfish,
> drifting behind them.** Both can share the panel; they are not the same character.

**CRABBY THE CRAB** — Round orange crab, suspicious eyes, one claw always tugging
downward. Usually in or near a bucket.

**BARRY THE BITTER BARRACUDA** — Long, sharp, dramatic. Dull steel-blue. Grumpy
romantic-tragedy energy. Holds a mug.

**NURSE SHARK** — Light grey nurse shark, white nurse cap with red cross, clipboard.
Kind but useless. Comic beat.

**KATYA "GOT YA" CATFISH** — Whiskered catfish with too-sweet smile, holds a mask or
mirror. Background only in Book #1.

**LENNY THE LEECH** — Small dark clingy leech attached to other characters' bags and
shells. Background only in Book #1.

**DEBBIE DOWNER** — Tired-eyed fish in an apron behind a café counter. Unimpressed.

## PROMPT TEMPLATE

Prepend to every page prompt for consistency:

> Children's picture book illustration, soft painterly digital art with visible brush
> texture, warm underwater lighting with golden god-rays, teal and aqua water with warm
> coral accents, rounded friendly character shapes, expressive faces, no text in image
> unless specified. Style consistent across a 32-page picture book.

Then append the per-page block below.

**Production note:** generate Wesley alone first (a turnaround: front, 3/4, side, sad
pose, proud pose) and use that sheet as a reference image for every subsequent page.
Same for Happy and Marcus. Character drift across 27 pages is the #1 failure mode.

## PAGE-BY-PAGE SHOT LIST

### Page 1 — Meet Wesley
**Text:** Hi! I'm Wesley… Wesley Negbaum. The most misunderstood manatee in the whole wide sea. Go ahead. Giggle. Everybody else does.
**Shot:** Close-up, Wesley center, waving one flipper directly at the reader, warm hopeful smile.
**Prompt:** WESLEY floating center frame, waving at the viewer, big warm smile, bright sunlit reef behind him, small background fish making exaggerated suspicious faces at him. Shallow depth, Wesley sharp, reef soft.
**Cameos:** none — establish the hero clean.

### Page 2 — Briny Barnacle Way
**Text:** I live on Briny Barnacle Way. The coral glows. The seaweed sways. The dolphins leap. The bubbles dance. Most mornings smell like salty sunshine. I love it here. Well… mostly.
**Shot:** Wide establishing shot of the undersea neighborhood.
**Prompt:** Wide undersea neighborhood, glowing coral houses, swaying seaweed gardens, dolphins arcing overhead, rising bubbles, a hand-painted wooden signpost reading BRINY BARNACLE WAY, WESLEY small and happy swimming through it.
**Cameos:** KATYA at a market booth labeled "Friendly Faces"; LENNY clinging to a passing fish's satchel; the YEAH-YEAHS as tiny distant shapes.

### Page 3 — The Negbaums
**Text:** My pa was a Negbaum. His pa was too. We've been Negbaums for as long as anyone remembers. Funny thing is… none of us were ever negative.
**Shot:** Warm sepia-toned interior. Wesley looking at framed family portraits.
**Prompt:** Warm golden interior of a coral home, WESLEY looking up at framed portraits of older manatees — one holding flowers, one helping a tiny fish, one smiling shyly. All look kind. Sepia-warm light, nostalgic, cozy.
**Cameos:** none.

### Page 4 — The reef decides
**Text:** But the reef didn't care. "Negbaum?" snorted a sardine. "Sounds negative." "Sounds gloomy." "Sounds grumpy." Nobody asked if I was kind… They heard my name and decided who I was.
**Shot:** Clusters of gossiping fish, Wesley pushed to the edge of frame.
**Prompt:** Groups of colorful reef fish whispering and pointing with exaggerated judgmental faces, WESLEY off to one side, small, hurt but still attempting a polite smile.
**Cameos:** NEGATIVE NANCY writing in a tiny notebook labeled "Needle Notes"; KATYA smiling too sweetly; LENNY attached to a tired-looking fish.

### Page 5 — A name can echo
**Text:** Maybe they wanted to defame my name because I wasn't the same. Or maybe they just didn't want to see me swim farther than they did… A name can echo. Especially underwater.
**Shot:** Wesley alone in open blue water. Isolation.
**Prompt:** WESLEY alone in vast open blue water, the word NEGBAUM repeating behind him in bubble-lettering, each repetition larger and fainter like an echo. Distant fish pointing. Cool desaturated palette. Lonely, spacious composition.
**Cameos:** none — let the loneliness breathe.

### Page 6 — The Salty Sourpusses
**Text:** Then came the Salty Sourpusses, slinking my way. And as soon as the Sourpusses joined in the fray, the rumors swirled and twirled through the sea. "Negbaum is negative!" "Just wait and see!"
**Shot:** Villain ensemble reveal. Storm-cloud composition.
**Prompt:** THE SALTY SOURPUSSES arriving as a dense grumpy cluster like a storm cloud made of fish, grey-green murky water around them, gossip bubbles twisting through the water like seaweed in a riptide. A glowing hanging sign reads DEBBIE'S DOWNERS DOWN UNDER.
**Cameos:** BARRY with his mug; CRABBY in a bucket; NELLY drifting behind; DEBBIE at the café door.

### Page 7 — The rules were easy
**Text:** The rules were easy. Whine. Complain. Blame. Repeat. "I did nothing wrong!" "It's everybody else's fault!" "Life isn't fair!" They wanted to be first of the worst. A school of fools swimming in circles.
**Shot:** Comic. A rules scroll plus fish literally swimming in a circle.
**Prompt:** An aged parchment scroll pinned to coral listing rules: WHINE / COMPLAIN / BLAME / REPEAT, with FIRST OF THE WORST in bold at the bottom. Behind it, a school of grumpy fish swimming in a tight pointless circle. Comic, theatrical.
**Cameos:** CRABBY with a small trophy reading "First of the Worst."

### Page 8 — The Yeah-Yeahs
**Text:** Then the Yeah-Yeahs showed up. "Yeah-yeah… don't try. Don't plan. Don't dream. You'll only fail." Negative Nelly drifted by. "Hope?" "Nope." Everybody laughed. Except me.
**Shot:** The cheer crew, and Nelly separate behind them.
**Prompt:** THE YEAH-YEAHS — small bright fish in matching sunglasses and caps — cheering with speech bubbles reading YEAH-YEAH!, positioned safely far from any actual risk. Behind them NEGATIVE NELLY the purple jellyfish drifts past with a bored expression and a small bubble reading "Nope."
**Cameos:** keep Nelly and the Yeah-Yeahs visually distinct — see consistency flag above.

### Page 9 — The seed of doubt
**Text:** At first… I laughed too. Then something strange happened. Their words started swimming around inside my head. Maybe they're right. Maybe my name really is negative. My smile floated away. A tiny seed of doubt drifted in.
**Shot:** Wesley close-up, thought-fish circling his head.
**Prompt:** Close on WESLEY, smile gone, eyes worried. Translucent grey thought-fish circle his head, each faintly labeled — "they're right," "what if I fail." His own smile drifts away from him as a small pale shape. Water dimming at the edges.
**Cameos:** none.

### Page 10 — Happy the Clam
**Text:** When I didn't know what to do, I swam to my best friend. Happy the Clam. "Happy," I said, "how are you always so happy?" Happy smiled. "Simple. I don't let every fish move into my shell."
**Shot:** Warm, safe, intimate. Contrast to everything before it.
**Prompt:** A calm sandy patch of seafloor, HAPPY THE CLAM open and glowing with warm pearl light, small smiling face inside. WESLEY leaning down close, tired, listening. Warm pool of light around them against cooler water beyond. Safe, cozy.
**Cameos:** far background — Sourpusses as tiny gossiping silhouettes.

### Page 11 — Clampdown Protocol
**Text:** Happy snapped shut. CLACK! "My shell protects me. And I protect my shell." Then he peeked back out. "If they loved you, they'd lift you." "If they dim your light, ditch it." CLACK!
**Shot:** Comic timing spread. Two clacks.
**Prompt:** HAPPY snapping shut with a bold hand-lettered CLACK! sound effect in the water, then a second smaller inset of him peeking back out mid-advice. WESLEY watching wide-eyed. Playful, punchy, comic-panel energy.
**Cameos:** none.

### Page 12 — Terrible training
**Text:** I tried. I really did. "Clampdown Protocol!" Nothing. Again. "Clampdown Protocol!" Still nothing. Turns out… being a manatee is terrible training for being a clam.
**Shot:** The funniest page in the book. Wesley failing physically.
**Prompt:** WESLEY comically attempting to curl into a clam shape — squeezed eyes, scrunched flippers, tail tucked, entirely unsuccessful. HAPPY watching from the sand, amused but kind. A faint CLACK! floating uselessly nearby.
**Cameos:** none. Let the joke land.

### Page 13 — Nurse Shark
**Text:** I tried everything. New shoes. New smile. New attitude. I even visited Nurse Shark. She said, "You've got a case of the blues." Nothing worked.
**Shot:** Clinic gag.
**Prompt:** An undersea clinic. NURSE SHARK in a white nurse cap with a red cross, holding a clipboard reading "Diagnosis: The Blues." WESLEY on an examination rock looking politely miserable. Pairs of tiny shoes floating away in the background.
**Cameos:** none.

### Page 14 — The hook
**Text:** The Sourpusses came back. "We told you." "Classic Negbaum." Their words wore smiles… but felt like hooks. Maybe they're right. What if I fail?
**Shot:** Lowest point. Sourpusses looming, Wesley small.
**Prompt:** THE SALTY SOURPUSSES crowding in around WESLEY with fake friendly smiles that read as cruel. Their speech bubbles are subtly hook-shaped. Wesley small, hunched, at his lowest. Murkiest, coolest water in the book.
**Cameos:** NANCY mid-needle; the YEAH-YEAHS at the back.

### Page 15 — The shadow
**Text:** Just then… the water became still. The little fish stopped talking. Even the seaweed seemed to listen. A giant shadow glided overhead.
**Shot:** Awe. Wide, quiet, reverent. Pull the camera way back.
**Prompt:** Wide reverent composition. The water goes glassy and still. A vast whale silhouette glides overhead, backlit by golden light shafts from the surface. Tiny fish frozen mid-swim below. Seaweed standing upright. WESLEY minuscule at the bottom of the frame looking up. Awe, silence, scale.
**Cameos:** NONE. This page is Marcus's.

### Page 16 — Marcus A. Whaleus
**Text:** Marcus A. Whaleus. He was calm as the deep, wise as the tide, and kind as a moonbeam.
**Shot:** Hero portrait of Marcus. Warm, safe, enormous.
**Prompt:** Portrait of MARCUS A. WHALEUS — large deep blue-grey whale with kind heavy-lidded eyes and a faint wise smile, wearing a draped mantle with a round medallion. Lit like moonlight. Fills most of the frame. Calm, benevolent, ancient.
**Cameos:** NONE.

### Page 17 — The obstacle is the way
**Text:** Marcus looked at me. Then he looked at the current. "Wesley," he said, "the obstacle… is the way." I blinked. "I don't understand." "I know," he smiled. "Yet."
**Shot:** Two-shot. The scale difference is the emotional content.
**Prompt:** MARCUS and WESLEY facing each other, enormous whale and small manatee. A visible current flows between them. The word NEGBAUM floats as a bubble between them while Marcus's gaze points past it toward open water. Intimate despite the scale gap.
**Cameos:** none.

### Page 18 — Ride the wave
**Text:** "Why fight the current when you can ride the wave?" "The harder the current, the stronger you swim." "Your choices shape your fate… not your name."
**Shot:** The biggest, most kinetic image in the book. Motion.
**Prompt:** A powerful curling wave of blue-white water dominating the frame. MARCUS gestures toward it. WESLEY beginning to move *with* the current rather than against it, body angled forward for the first time in the book. Dynamic, energetic, hopeful. Brightest page so far.
**Cameos:** none.

### Page 19 — Three things
**Text:** That night… I started writing. Three things I was grateful for. Every morning. Every night. I smiled. Even when I didn't feel like smiling. I whispered, "Yes… I can."
**Shot:** Quiet, warm, intimate interior. The turn.
**Prompt:** Night interior. WESLEY at a small desk writing in an open journal by warm lamplight — visible list: "1. My family 2. Happy 3. The ocean." A small bubble reads "Yes, I can." HAPPY dozing half-open nearby. Deep blue night water outside, gold light inside. Tender, cozy, hopeful.
**Cameos:** MARCUS as a distant peaceful silhouette outside.

### Page 20 — A single drop
**Text:** One little choice. Every day. One kind thought. One grateful heart. One brave swim. Marcus was right. A little every day becomes a lot. Just like… a mighty wave begins with a single drop.
**Shot:** Symbolic. Minimal. Give it space.
**Prompt:** Minimal composition. A single drop falling into still water, sending out concentric rings. Beside it a tiny green sprout growing from the seafloor. Soft, quiet, lots of negative space. Symbolic rather than literal.
**Cameos:** none.

### Page 21 — Who's Got It Best?
**Text:** The Sourpusses returned. "Who's Got It Worse?" they shouted. I smiled. "I'd rather play… Who's Got It Best?" The whole reef went quiet.
**Shot:** The turn, in public. Wesley calm and centered for the first time.
**Prompt:** THE SOURPUSSES crowding in holding a battered sign reading WHO'S GOT IT WORSE?, while WESLEY calmly holds up a bright new hand-painted sign reading WHO'S GOT IT BEST? He is upright, centered, and serene. The surrounding reef fish are visibly startled. Light shifting warmer.
**Cameos:** NANCY, NELLY, BARRY, CRABBY, YEAH-YEAHS all visible, skeptical, not yet won over.

### Page 22 — It stopped sticking
**Text:** When they complained… I encouraged. When they blamed… I took responsibility. When they doubted… I practiced gratitude. Then something funny happened. The negativity… stopped sticking.
**Shot:** Wesley glowing. Gloom sliding off him.
**Prompt:** WESLEY upright and radiant with a soft warm glow at his chest, grey gloom-bubbles visibly sliding off and away from him without landing. Surrounding fish reacting with surprise. Warm gold breaking through the blue.
**Cameos:** softening Sourpusses at the edges.

### Page 23 — What my name meant
**Text:** That's when I realized… I didn't need to change my name. I needed to change what my name meant.
**Shot:** Quiet realization. Warm and still.
**Prompt:** WESLEY alone in a warm golden wash of light, calm and self-possessed, looking slightly upward. The word NEGBAUM behind him shifting from cold grey to warm gold. Simple, still, triumphant without shouting.
**Cameos:** none.

### Page 24 — Negbaum's
**Text:** Years later… a little shop opened on Briny Barnacle Way. The sign read: NEGBAUM'S — Neg Be Gone with Negbaum's. The same fish who laughed at my name couldn't wait to tell their friends about it. Funny how that works.
**Shot:** Warm storefront wide shot. The payoff.
**Prompt:** A warm glowing undersea shop with a striped awning, shelves of bright bottles, and a large hand-painted sign reading NEGBAUM'S with smaller lettering "Neg Be Gone with Negbaum's!" A long happy line of reef creatures out front. Golden lantern light. Festive, warm, busy.
**Cameos:** the full future-book roster in line — BARRY "just researching," NANCY reading the label suspiciously, NELLY cautiously hopeful, KATYA in a fake mustache, LENNY asking about free samples, YEAH-YEAHS with harmless YEAH! signs.

### Page 25 — Annoyingly nice
**Text:** They spritzed once. They smiled twice. Even the crab said, "Annoyingly nice."
**Shot:** Comic close group scene.
**Prompt:** Reef creatures trying the spray, delighted. WESLEY holding up a bottle proudly. CRABBY in the foreground sniffing the mist with an expression of deep irritation at how much he likes it, speech bubble: "Annoyingly nice."
**Cameos:** as above, closer.

### Page 26 — I owned it
**Text:** The same name they laughed at before was painted bright above my store. Not because I changed it. Because I owned it. Not because they approved. Because I stopped asking.
**Shot:** Hero shot. Wesley under his own sign.
**Prompt:** Low hero angle. WESLEY beneath the large glowing NEGBAUM'S sign, proud, calm, kind. Warm golden light on the sign and on him. The strongest, most confident image of Wesley in the book.
**Cameos:** none — this is his.

### Page 27 — Absolutely positive
**Text:** I was still Wesley. Still a Negbaum. Still smiling. But now I knew: my name could not steer me. Their words could not own me. Their doubt could not define me. Only my choices could. So if someone says, "Negbaum sounds negative," I just smile and say, "I'm absolutely positive."
**Shot:** Wesley to camera. Full circle from page 1.
**Prompt:** WESLEY facing the viewer, mirroring his page-1 pose but transformed — upright, radiant, genuinely joyful. Bright celebratory reef around him. HAPPY and MARCUS visible behind him. A parchment scroll in-frame reads "Because your choices shape your fate… not your name."
**Cameos:** the whole reef, softened Sourpusses included.

### Page 31 (back matter) — Closing moral
**Text:** Don't judge a friend by their name or their frown. Sometimes all they need is a chance to turn things around.
**Shot:** Full-width celebration band.
**Prompt:** Wide celebration scene. WESLEY, HAPPY, MARCUS and the full reef community gathered around a large GOOD, BETTER, BEST sign. Background signs read "Who's Got It Worse? Closed for Repairs," "Choose the Good Current," "Neg Be Gone." Every future-book character visible around the edges, softened but not fully changed.
**Cameos:** everyone.

## PRODUCTION ORDER

1. **Character reference sheets first** — Wesley turnaround (5 poses), Happy (open/shut/peeking), Marcus (portrait + full body). These become the reference images for everything else.
2. **Then the three anchor pages** — 1, 15, 26. If those three hold together stylistically, the book will.
3. **Then the gloom block** (4–14) in one sitting, so the desaturated palette stays consistent.
4. **Then the hope block** (18–27) in one sitting, same reason.
5. **Then the connectors** (2, 3, 16, 17).
6. **Cameos last** — add on a second pass over finished pages, so they don't fight the composition.

## QUALITY GATE (before layout)
- [ ] Wesley recognizably the same character on all 27 pages
- [ ] Wesley's posture arc reads: droops p4–14, turns p19, rises p21–27
- [ ] Palette shifts cool→warm across the book
- [ ] Every page has a clear text-safe area (see kdp-spec.md)
- [ ] Nelly and the Yeah-Yeahs visually distinct
- [ ] No unintended text/gibberish lettering in any generated image
