# Book 1 Background Cameo Plan — Easter Eggs for Upcoming Books

**Core rule:** Future characters appear in Book #1 backgrounds as silent visual Easter eggs.
They do not steal the scene, do not explain themselves, and should feel discoverable on a
second or third reading. Child notices something fun; parent notices the setup; the series
quietly builds its world.

## Page-by-Page Placement (master manuscript page numbers)
- **Title page:** Crabby (bucket), Nelly (Hope? Nope), Barry (mug), Yeah-Yeahs (banner), Katya (mask).
- **Page 2 (Briny Barnacle Way):** Katya at market booth; Lenny on a satchel; Yeah-Yeahs distant.
- **Page 4 (reef judging):** Nancy whispering with Needle Notes; Katya fake-sweet; Lenny on a tired fish.
- **Page 6 (Sourpusses enter):** Nelly drifting; Barry with mug; Crabby in bucket; Debbie at café door.
- **Page 9 (Debbie's Downers Down Under):** richest Easter-egg spread — Debbie, Barry, Crabby, Nelly, Nancy all present with props.
- **Page 10 (Who's Got It Worse?):** Crabby with "First of the Worst" trophy; Nelly half-floating; Nancy needling with a fake compliment.
- **Page 11 (invite):** Yeah-Yeahs with tiny signs: DO IT / YEAH-YEAH / BAD IDEA.
- **Page 15 (Wesley → Happy):** Sourpusses tiny gossiping silhouettes; Lenny clinging "helpfully."
- **Page 21 (Marcus arrives):** NO cameos — this moment belongs to Marcus.
- **Page 30 (Sourpusses return):** Nancy, Nelly, Crabby, Barry, Yeah-Yeahs watching, skeptical, not transformed.
- **Page 34 (reef plays Good Better Best):** Nelly cautiously offering gratitude; Crabby pretending not to enjoy it; Barry rolling eyes but listening; Nancy taking softer notes; Yeah-Yeahs confused.
- **Page 35:** Crabby almost smiling; Barry hiding a grin behind his mug; Nelly surprised by a positive thought; Nancy pausing mid-needle.
- **Page 36:** one unnamed Sourpuss admits gratitude; future characters watch. Reef can change, but each major character still needs their own book.
- **Page 42 (reef apology):** Katya uncomfortable (truth is not her favorite snack); Lenny detaching; Nelly peeking from kelp.
- **Page 50 (Negbaum's shop):** big series payoff — Barry "just researching," Crabby "Annoyingly nice," Nancy reading label suspiciously, Nelly smelling with cautious hope, Katya in fake mustache, Lenny asking for samples, Yeah-Yeahs with harmless YEAH! signs.
- **Final page:** ALL future characters around the edges, softened but not fixed. Signs: Good, Better, Best / Neg Be Gone / Choose the Good Current / Who's Got It Worse? Closed for Repairs.

## Art Direction Note
Keep cameos subtle. The main story must remain Wesley's. Future characters should appear
like hidden shells in the reef: recognizable, funny, discoverable, not distracting.
Children point and ask, "Who is that?" Parents think, "Ah, here comes trouble."
