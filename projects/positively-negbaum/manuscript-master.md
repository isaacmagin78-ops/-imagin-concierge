## Visual Style
Bright, whimsical underwater picture-book style (matches the three concept storyboard sheets from 2026-08-14). Big expressions, soft rounded character shapes, layered reef backgrounds, visible currents, playful bubble typography, parent-facing visual gags in signs/labels/background objects.

## Character Notes
- **Wesley Negbaum:** Young manatee. Round, gentle, expressive. Starts uncertain and droopy; becomes upright, calm, bright.
- **Salty Sourpusses:** Antagonist group. Sour, theatrical, gossipy, funny rather than frightening. Swirl around Wesley like a negative current.
- **Happy the Clam:** Small, blunt, loyal. Pearl glow inside shell. Comedic CLACK moments.
- **Marcus A. Whaleus:** Huge wise whale, calm and moonlike. Slow, grounded, majestic movement.
