The bucket with a hole

The last page I made was too complicated. Here is the same thing, drawn.

1 — TWO KINDS OF THINGS

Some stuff you rent. Some stuff you own.

All the AI stuff we plug in is rented. It's great, and in six months every guy on the internet has the exact same box. Being good at renting doesn't put you ahead of anybody.

What you own is thirty years of knowing how a room works. What to wear. What gets you turned away at the door. Who's actually who. That is the whole business.

2 — HERE'S THE PROBLEM

Right now that knowledge pours straight out of a hole.

You already built the thing that does this. It's called room-read. You ask what to wear, it tells you, and it's good.

But when the conversation ends, it forgets everything. Next week you ask again and I start from nothing. Thirty years goes in the top and runs straight out the hole.

You've been paying for the water and keeping none of it.

3 — THE FIX

Plug the hole. That's the whole idea.

Every time I give you a room read, I also write it down in one file. The place. The door rule. What you wore. What was current that night.

Nothing new to build. No new project. It is one extra step added to something that already works.

In a year that file is a book on how the rich world actually works, and there is exactly one copy of it on earth. That's the thing people pay for.

4 — GOOD NEWS

Two things are already running without you.

Staying ahead of the news is handled. A little robot on GitHub checks twice a day and writes down what it finds. No key, no button, nobody has to be awake. It's been running since 16 Aug.

Your posting is not dead. I checked live today — 20 posts are scheduled, the next one goes out this afternoon, across TikTok, Instagram, Threads and YouTube. The old note saying the queue was empty is out of date.

So I only need one yes from you.

Should I add the saving step to room-read, so from now on every room you walk into gets kept instead of thrown away?

It's a small change to one file. Nothing else moves, nothing breaks, and it can be undone.

Checked live today, 18 Aug 2026: the posting schedule and credits, the news robot, and the room-read file. The one number I could not re-check is the $1.29 of lifetime earnings — that's from your notes on 14 Aug, because Amazon isn't connected here.
