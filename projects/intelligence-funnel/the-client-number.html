What this is really asking

Not how many people you'd like. How many could be texting you — "I'm going to this thing Thursday, what am I walking into?" — before it stops being good.

Fewer people means a higher price. A higher price means a different kind of person walks through the door. That's the whole trade, and it's why the number comes before the price.
