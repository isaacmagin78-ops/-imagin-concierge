| Fact | Source |

⚠️ **The catch.** The exemption requires the agent **not accept fees or commissions directly
from clients** — everything flows through the host agency. That collides with charging his own
trip-design fee. The structure must separate a **concierge/intelligence membership** billed
directly from **travel bookings** compensated through the host. **Needs a compliance answer
from a Florida attorney or the host agency before a dollar is charged for trip planning.**

## THE HYBRID — **Room Read**

The skill built on 16 Aug is the product. One name, one thing.

> *A private membership that tells you how the room works before you walk into it — and books
> the trip that gets you there.*

**Layer 1 — free front door.** The adaptive diagnostic, rebuilt to the alive/sci-fi/fun standard.
Its job is **not** volume capture; it is to qualify a few of the right people and let them feel the
intelligence in ninety seconds. It ends by giving a real room read for one real event they name —
before any contact details. That screen is the entire sales argument and no competitor can copy it.

**Layer 2 — the membership (recurring money).** Priced in the Quintessentially entry band, not
Knightsbridge, because he is one person. Unlimited room reads, event prep, situational
intelligence on a phone ten minutes before the door.

**Layer 3 — travel (the destination, arriving last).** Host agency, $51.50 exemption, commissions
on member bookings — structured so travel compensation flows through the host and the membership
fee is separately for intelligence and access. Bespoke travel is where this ends up, exactly as
recorded three times, but as layer three so the 6–12 month commission ramp never carries the business.

**Underneath all three: the corpus.** Every room read writes itself down. Year one it is a
service; year three it is the asset, and what lets the software answer without him.

**Why this wins:** sells the half nobody can buy, gives away the half machines do better; fee beats
commission ~25× per relationship on sourced numbers; needs no staff; gives the funnel a job matching
its strength; accumulates by construction; keeps travel as the destination without betting year one on it.

**First cash is separate.** The **Collection Record** is written, needs no code, and is the only
thing here sellable this month at $3,500. It is also a legitimate reason to talk to Arturo, and the
one thing offerable to Linda as a gift rather than an ask.
